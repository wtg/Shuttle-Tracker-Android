# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 21, 2026

Shuttle Tracker ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle
your information.

## Location

The App may access your device location in order to:

- Show you the closest shuttle stop.
- Calculate how far you are from that stop.

We do not collect, store, or transmit your location data. All location processing happens
locally on your device, in real time, and is never logged or sent to our servers.

## Push notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver optional service announcements (e.g. route
changes, delays). To enable this, your device is assigned a Firebase registration token, which
is sent to Firebase's servers - this is what makes it possible to reach your device with a
notification. We do not use this token for anything beyond delivering these notifications, and
we do not attach any other personal information to it. You can decline the notification
permission during setup, or disable it later in your device's system settings, without losing
any other functionality of the App.

## Sharing of information

We do not share your location or any other personal information with third parties, beyond what
is necessary for Firebase Cloud Messaging (operated by Google) to deliver push notifications as
described above.

## Children's privacy

Shuttle Tracker does not knowingly collect, store, or share personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update it here and revise the "Effective
Date" above.
